我的远方 / MY DISTANT HORIZON

我从城市出发，最后停在了一群牦牛身边。I LEFT THE CITY AND STOPPED BESIDE A HERD OF YAKS.

2017年硕士毕业，我去了北京。后来回到安庆，又在杭州住了一年，再开着一辆车，走过中国的许多城市。让我留下来的，不只是风景，是这里的人。

After graduate school and Beijing, I returned to Anqing, lived in Hangzhou, then crossed China by car. A landscape drew me in; relationships made me stay.
