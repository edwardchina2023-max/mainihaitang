这是一位老朋友笔下的黄海——相识五年后的一次重逢。

Not Huang Hai’s own account, but an old friend’s view of him—written at a reunion five years after they met.

01

一次重逢A REUNION

他们2020年相识于杭州。五年后再见，旧友眼前的黄海黑了、瘦了，留起了长发，在高原与城市之间来来回回。故事从这次重逢讲起，而不是从一段刻意的传奇。

The writer first met Huang Hai in Hangzhou in 2020. Five years later, the reunion begins with visible change: thinner, darker from the sun, moving between plateau and city.

02

一辆车与很多城市ONE CAR, MANY CITIES

文章回望北京、杭州，和2021年开始的那趟自驾。旅行在这里是一种行动，而不是答案：黄海在许多地方想过停留，最终把他带回理塘的，是那里的人。

Beijing, Hangzhou and a long drive across China form the road into the story. Travel is presented as action rather than an answer; relationships, not scenery alone, bring him back.

03

牧场的代价THE COST OF A PASTURE

写到牦牛，文章没有回避损失、经验不足，和一门尚未走通的生意。文中的牛群数量与收支，是作者2025年写下时的记录。

The pasture chapter does not hide loss, inexperience or an unfinished business model. Its herd and financial figures belong to the article’s 2025 reporting.

04

旷野不是答案OPEN COUNTRY IS NOT AN ANSWER

文章最后，回到城市里的一次告别。所谓旷野，并不是对城市的否定，而是作者对选择、对有限的时间、对人生不同走法的理解。

The story ends back in the city. Open country is not a rejection of urban life, but the writer’s way of thinking about choice, finite time and divergent paths.

报道中的时间线 / REPORTED TIMELINE

一段履历，被写成了一条路。A LIFE BECOMES A ROAD.

  1. 2017

    研究生毕业后开始北漂。Graduate school to Beijing.

  2. 2020—2021

    在杭州参与茶室与人文课程活动。A teahouse and cultural programs in Hangzhou.

  3. 2021

    独自驾车开始环游中国。The drive across China begins.

  4. 后来

    返回理塘，牧场成为生活的重要部分。Returning to Litang; the pasture becomes central.
